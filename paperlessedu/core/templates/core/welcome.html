Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setup.

Common questions school proprietors and headmasters ask before making the transition.

How does the onboarding and school setup process work?

We handle the technical setup for you. Our team visits your campus, configures your classes, subjects, grading thresholds, and term calendars, helps digitize your current student rosters, and trains your teaching and administration staff in person.

Do our teachers need high-end computers or special equipment?

No. PaperlessEdu runs smoothly in any standard web browser on existing laptops, desktop computers, tablets, or smartphones. Teachers can take daily roll call or submit marks directly from their mobile phones without installing large applications.

What happens if our school internet connection is unstable?

PaperlessEdu is engineered for low-bandwidth conditions. Page payloads are lightweight, and asset caching keeps class lists and rosters accessible even during temporary connectivity dips.

Can we customize report cards with our school logo and grading scale?

Yes. Every school deployment includes your school crest, motto, headmaster signature space, and tailored grading schemes (percentages, letter grades, WAEC-style scales, or early-childhood rating rubrics).

How is our school's student and financial data protected?

PaperlessEdu uses a single-tenant deployment model where each school's database is strictly isolated. All connections require HTTPS encryption, sensitive financial data is protected by strict permission checks, and automated daily backups ensure no school records are ever lost.
